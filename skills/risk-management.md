Risk management (Article 9) is a governance dimension: it is satisfied by a
control/process, not by a test tool, and it runs across the whole lifecycle.

Adequate coverage requires evidence of:
- A **structured risk-identification** step covering risks to health, safety,
  and fundamental rights, with a classification/prioritisation method.
- **Mitigations traced to identified risks**, and residual risk recorded and
  accepted by a named owner.
- **Post-market monitoring** feeding new evidence back into the risk register.
- Linkage to the other dimensions' findings (a robustness or fairness gap is a
  risk-register entry, not a separate universe).

Because this dimension has controls but no tests, it cannot be marked "covered"
without an accepted risk-management control checklist — the deterministic floor
enforces this for high-risk systems.
